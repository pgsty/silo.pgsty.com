<a id="deployment-checklists"></a>

The following checklists provide a high-level guideline for validating production-readiness of MinIO deployments.

These checklists may not meet the precise requirements of your unique deployment topology or architecture, and are intended as a best-effort guide to reliable production deployments.

Plan deployment and architecture reviews around your workload, recovery objectives, and measured capacity. For assistance, see [SILO troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshooting/).

Checklists:

- [Hardware Checklist](/operations/checklists/hardware/#minio-hardware-checklist)
- [Security Checklist](/operations/checklists/security/#minio-security-checklist)
- [Software Checklist](/operations/checklists/software/#minio-software-checklists)
